# ForeverNet — English

**Professions, cooperative crafting, and item requests for World of Warcraft: Forever.**

ForeverNet helps you plan crafts and find assistance within your guild or group. Choose a recipe and quantity to calculate materials, account for your stock, and build a production chain with potential crafters.

## Features

- **Recipe catalog.** Scan an open profession, save learned recipes, and search for items.
- **Production chains.** See which components need crafting, the order of production, and which network members know suitable recipes.
- **Stock tracking.** Plans account for items you carry and saved personal bank contents.
- **Automatic bank scanning.** Bank data updates when you visit your personal bank and change its contents. Snapshots are stored separately for each character.
- **Help requests.** Publish a request for an item or missing component. Other ForeverNet users can offer assistance.
- **In-game sharing.** Profiles and requests travel through guild or group channels, without a separate external server.
- **Blueprint and Camping support.** Manually mark Blueprint recipes and record camp facility requirements.
- **WoW-style interface.** Familiar game frames, item icons, colored material cards, and a movable minimap button.
- **Localization.** English and Russian interfaces, with automatic language selection and a manual setting.

## Getting Started

1. Open ForeverNet using the minimap button or `/fn`.
2. Open your profession and click **Scan**. Clear the profession window’s search and filters first to include more recipes.
3. Choose a recipe, enter a quantity, and click **Build chain**.
4. Visit your personal bank to save its contents for planning.
5. To cooperate with others, have your guild or group members install ForeverNet, enable **Sharing**, and click **Sync**.
6. Select a missing component and click **Ask for help**.

## Controls

- `/fn` — open the main window.
- `/fn settings` — settings and addon information.
- `/fn help` — command reference.
- `/fn demo` — sample production chain.

**Minimap button:** left-click to toggle the window, right-click for help. Drag the button to reposition it; its position is saved.

## Current Status

ForeverNet is in early development and targets **WoW: Forever 1.60.1**.

The planner suggests a crafting sequence but does not guarantee an optimal route or player availability. Players craft, travel, and trade manually. The request owner’s addon automatically accepts the first offer of help.

Bank contents outside a bank visit come from the last saved snapshot. Your bank inventory is never shared with other players. Profile sharing is disabled on first installation and requires user activation; network synchronization is started manually.

Automatic detection of all Blueprint and Camping mechanics is not yet implemented.
